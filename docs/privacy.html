PuttLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

PuttLog is a disc golf putting-practice tracker. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it's handled.

Summary

PuttLog does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything you record stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Putts you log (distance, make/miss, and timestamp) and your app preferences (distance unit, quick-entry range, audio input setting) are stored locally on your device only, using standard Android local storage (Room database and DataStore). This data is never sent anywhere. You can delete it at any time from Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Microphone / voice input

Voice input is an optional setting, off by default. When you turn it on, PuttLog uses Android's built-in, on-device speech recognizer to convert your speech ("20 make", "15 miss") into a logged putt. Audio is processed entirely on your device by the operating system's speech recognition service — PuttLog does not record, store, or transmit audio, and no audio or transcript data leaves your device.

Permissions the app requests

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — only used while voice input is active, to enable the on-device speech recognizer described above.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH) — used only to route audio through a connected Bluetooth headset while voice input is active.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and Foreground Service (Microphone) — Android requires a visible notification while an app is actively listening via the microphone in the background; this is shown only while voice input is running.

Children's privacy

PuttLog does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.
